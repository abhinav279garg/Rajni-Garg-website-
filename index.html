Meet Rajni ji

A maker who finds possibility in a simple thread.

Rajni Garg creates small, joyful pieces from cord and knotwork in Sagar, Madhya Pradesh.

Her photographs show the real rhythm of making: choosing colours, repeating a knot until it sits just right, adding a painted face or bead, and preparing every finished piece with care.

This website brings that work together so family, friends, curious learners and future customers can discover it without losing the warmth of a personal conversation.

A closer look

A few knots. A tiny face. A whole personality.

The knot doll begins with cord and repetition. A wooden bead becomes a face, a small clip makes it useful, and colour gives each character its mood. No two handmade expressions need to feel exactly alike.
